Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: November 22, 2026

1. Institutional Scope & Commitment

ClassMarker ("we", "our", or "the Platform") provides attendance verification, academic roster management, and statutory exam compliance services to higher education institutions, academic faculty members, and enrolled students. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and digital rights of our institutional users and students under applicable data protection frameworks, including the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and international standards.

2. Information We Collect

To provide reliable academic record-keeping, the Platform collects the following limited categories of information:

  • Student Identity Data: Full Name, Matriculation / Student Registration Number, Academic Department, Course Enrolment, and Institutional Email address (where provided).
  • Class Attendance Records: Timestamps of check-in, course session identifiers, lecture topics, and attendance verification status.
  • Geolocation & Satellite Telemetry: Physical geographic coordinates (Latitude, Longitude) and satellite accuracy estimates captured strictly at the moment a student submits attendance.
  • Device Telemetry & Fingerprint: Browser User-Agent, screen dimensions, client timestamp, and a cryptographic hardware hash generated locally on the device to prevent proxy check-in fraud.

3. Strict GPS Geolocation Privacy Standards

We recognize that student location data is sensitive. ClassMarker enforces the following strict safeguards:

  • Zero Continuous Tracking: GPS coordinates are queried strictly once when the student clicks "Submit Attendance". The platform NEVER monitors or tracks location before or after check-in.
  • Distance-Only Storage: Coordinates are evaluated in real-time against the lecturer's lecture hall perimeter to calculate proximity distance.
  • No Commercial Monetization: Location data is never sold, shared with advertising networks, or used for behavioral profiling.

4. How We Use Academic Information

Collected information is used exclusively to:

  • Verify genuine physical presence in designated university lecture venues.
  • Prevent proxy attendance, mock location spoofing, and duplicate submissions.
  • Calculate student semester attendance percentages under the statutory 75% exam compliance standard.
  • Generate certified academic attendance rosters for departmental heads, deans, and university registries.

5. Data Retention & Security

All student records and attendance logs are encrypted in transit via SSL/TLS (HTTPS) and stored in secure cloud infrastructure with automated database backups. Academic records are maintained for the duration of the institutional semester and academic session as mandated by university senate retention guidelines.

6. Platform Development & Contact

ClassMarker is engineered and maintained by e65web.com.ng. For privacy inquiries, institutional compliance requests, or data rectification, please contact our data governance desk at privacy@e65web.com.ng.
