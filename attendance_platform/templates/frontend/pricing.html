Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from lecturers, HODs, and academic registries.

Do students need to download an application from the Play Store or App Store?

No. ClassMarker is 100% web-based. Students scan the QR code using their regular phone camera or open the link in any mobile browser (Safari, Chrome, etc.) and mark attendance in seconds.

What happens if a student has no data or their phone is dead?

ClassMarker includes a dedicated "Roll-Call Fallback" bar right on the lecturer's monitor. The lecturer simply enters the student's matriculation number to mark them present with a special roll-call audit tag.

How does the 75% exam compliance formula work?

The platform tracks total lecture sessions held for the course. Each student's verified check-ins are divided by the total sessions. Students reaching 75% or higher receive an official "Eligible" badge and green status on the exported certified Excel report.
