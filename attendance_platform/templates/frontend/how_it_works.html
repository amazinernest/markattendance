How ClassMarker Works for Lecturers & Students

Eliminate wasted class time, roll-call disputes, and proxy signing. Here is the step-by-step walkthrough of how ClassMarker works on lecture day.

👨‍🏫

For Academic Faculty

Fast, effortless session launching in seconds

Lecturer Flow
1

Upload Roster & Create Session

Upload your course roster from Excel, CSV, or PDF. Enter your lecture topic (e.g. "Lecture 4: Standardization & Norms") to give every class meeting distinct academic reference.

2

Lock GPS & Project QR (or Print Poster)

Click "📍 Use My Current Location" to lock the classroom geofence. Project the QR code on the lecture hall screen or click "Print QR Poster" to post an A4 sign at the entrance door.

3

Monitor Live Feed & Export 75% Audit

Watch incoming check-ins stream live with physical proximity distance (e.g. "12m away • Verified Phone"). End the session in 1 click or download semester clearance reports for the registry.

🎓

For Enrolled Students

Zero apps, zero logins, instant digital proof

Student Flow
1

Point Camera & Scan QR Code

Students simply open their phone's built-in camera and scan the projected QR code or entrance poster. Opens instantly in their mobile browser with no app downloads required.

2

Enter Matric Number & Verify Presence

The student enters their university matriculation number. The system verifies their enrolment against the official class list and checks physical proximity via GPS.

3

Receive Cryptographic Attendance Receipt

The student receives an instant digital receipt showing timestamp, lecture topic, and verified badge. Their phone is locked to their matriculation number for that session.

Automated Defense Architecture

How Cheating & Proxy Signing Are Blocked

ClassMarker uses multi-layered forensic verification to guarantee that only physically present students are recorded.

🚫 1. Hostel & Off-Campus Fraud
Mathematical Haversine Distance Check

If an absent student in their room receives the QR code from a friend via WhatsApp, the server computes the exact distance from the lecturer's locked venue. If greater than the radius (e.g. 100m), check-in is instantly denied.

🛰️ 2. Mock GPS & Fake Location Apps
Telemetry & Satellite Hardware Validation

Fake GPS apps create synthetic fixes with zero atmospheric jitter or trigger Android mock provider flags. ClassMarker detects these anomalies, rejects synthetic coordinate injection, and requires genuine GPS hardware.

📱 3. Device Sharing (Signing for Friends)
Hardware Fingerprint Device Lock

A present student cannot take attendance on their phone for 5 absent roommates. Each device generates a persistent hardware fingerprint; once a student checks in, subsequent attempts on that phone are blocked.
