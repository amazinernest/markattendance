Features

Engineered for Academic Integrity & Operational Reality

Every feature was built to address real-world classroom challenges: proxy signing, poor Wi-Fi, and multi-department lectures.

GPS Classroom Geofencing

Prevents absent students from checking in from hostels or off-campus. The server calculates accurate physical distance using the Haversine formula and rejects any submission outside the classroom radius.

Printable Classroom Posters

Teaching in an amphitheatre without a working projector? Generate an official, ink-friendly A4/Letter QR poster in 1 click featuring course details, lecture topic, and anti-tamper timestamps.

Multi-Department Merges

Handle courses taken jointly by multiple departments (e.g. GST courses or Engineering Math). Upload departmental lists separately without overwriting existing students.

Peer Assistance Kiosk Mode

Students whose phone batteries died or who lack data can check in on a classmate's device using Peer Kiosk Mode, tagged transparently in the audit stream so no student is excluded.

Distinct Per-Class QR Codes

Every lecture meeting generates a unique cryptographic UUID token. A photo or screenshot of a past QR code will never work for today's lecture topic.

75% Statutory Exam Auditing

Automates institutional regulations requiring 75% minimum lecture attendance to sit for semester examinations, generating certified Excel and PDF reports with zero manual math.

About Us

Designed for Academic Rigor, Simplicity, and Inclusivity

Attendly was founded with a singular purpose: to modernize the archaic attendance procedures that plague higher education. In lecture halls across universities, valuable instruction time is lost passing around fragile paper sheets that are easily falsified with proxy signatures.

Proprietary hardware solutions—such as wall-mounted biometric fingerprint scanners—routinely suffer from hardware breakdowns, long queues, high maintenance costs, and vendor lock-in.

We built Attendly as pure software infrastructure that transforms the smartphone already in every student's pocket into an authenticated, location-verified academic credential.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding security, offline support, and institutional deployment.

How does GPS Geofencing stop students from sending QR screenshots to friends?

When geofencing is enabled, the check-in form requires the student's mobile device GPS. The server calculates physical distance to the classroom using the Haversine formula. If a student is at home or in their hostel (e.g. 800m away), the submission is strictly blocked with an Out of Range error.

Do students need to install an app from Google Play or App Store?

No. Attendly is built with zero render-blocking dependencies and loads in under 2 seconds on any mobile browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox). There are zero app downloads, installations, or account logins required for students.

How do students without a browsing phone or active data check in?

Attendly provides two built-in fallbacks: Peer Assistance Kiosk mode, which allows a classmate with data to enter their peer's matric number on the same device, and Lecturer Manual Roll-Call, allowing the instructor to mark present directly from their dashboard.

Can multiple departments taking the same lecture be merged?

Yes. Courses can receive multiple separate roster uploads across different departments (e.g. Computer Science, Mechanical Engineering, and Physics taking General Chemistry). Attendly merges them cleanly without duplication.
